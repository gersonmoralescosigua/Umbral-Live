THE INVESTIGATOR’S NOTEBOOK

01 / Observe before interpreting

Describe what you can actually see or hear. Note the time, weather and light conditions. Keep your observations separate from your explanation.

02 / Invite another explanation

Could a reflection, aircraft, animal or recording artifact explain the signal? Invite the room to challenge your theory. Uncertainty is useful evidence.

03 / Leave the place as you found it

Get permission before entering a location. Protect private addresses and identities. A compelling transmission never requires putting yourself or someone else at risk.
